sections:
  - heading: The idea
    body: >-
      Most fitness apps ask how many calories you burned. VedThenics asks
      whether you completed today's practice. Ancient Indian thought often
      treated body, mind, nourishment and character as connected — VedThenics
      applies that holistic idea to modern calisthenics, as one intentional
      routine rather than another workout tracker chasing streaks and
      dopamine.
  - heading: The product
    body: >-
      A week organized around Workout, Recovery & Mobility, Stretch, and Run
      Day. Practice is logged set by set, skills progress through staged
      self-assessment, and each session can close with optional Intention,
      Manas, Ahara and Dharma reflection — never required, always available.
  - heading: Four pillars
    body: >-
      Sharira (Body) — strength, skills, mobility, stretch, recovery and
      running, with optional Apple Health import. Manas (Mind) — short
      mindfulness and breathing. Ahara (Nourishment) — simple reminders of
      nutritional philosophy, no calorie counting. Dharma (Character) —
      twelve rotating reflection prompts, answer or skip.
  - heading: Philosophy
    body: >-
      Built for long-term practice, not short-term motivation: an honest
      streak earned by completing planned practice, no freezes, no
      pay-to-protect. On-device insights — nothing leaves the phone for
      analytics. Free, with no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases.
      Apple Health is optional and never blocks core practice.
---
VedThenics is Nuvya's first product — a philosophy-first calisthenics practice OS for iPhone, built for a disciplined, long-term practice rather than a scoreboard.
