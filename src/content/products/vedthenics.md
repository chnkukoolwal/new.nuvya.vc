sections:
  - heading: Why it exists
    body: >-
      Most fitness apps ask how many calories you burned. VedThenics asks
      whether you completed today's practice. Ancient Indian thought often
      treated body, mind, nourishment and character as connected — VedThenics
      applies that holistic idea to modern calisthenics, as one intentional
      routine rather than another workout tracker chasing streaks and
      dopamine.
  - heading: What it feels like
    body: >-
      Calm, not naggy. A daily practice, not a scoreboard. Progress is
      earned honestly — no streak freezes, no pay-to-protect, nothing
      gamified into anxiety. Insights stay on-device; nothing leaves the
      phone for analytics. Free, with no accounts, no ads and no in-app
      purchases. Apple Health is optional and never blocks core practice.
  - heading: How it works
    body: >-
      The week is organized around Workout, Recovery & Mobility, Stretch,
      and Run Day, built on four pillars — Sharira (Body): strength,
      skills, mobility and running, with optional Apple Health import.
      Manas (Mind): short mindfulness and breathing. Ahara (Nourishment):
      simple reminders of nutritional philosophy, no calorie counting.
      Dharma (Character): twelve rotating reflection prompts, answer or
      skip. Practice is logged set by set, skills progress through staged
      self-assessment — reflection is always optional, never required.
